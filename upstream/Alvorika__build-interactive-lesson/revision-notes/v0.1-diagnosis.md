# v0.1 regression diagnosis

## Evidence reviewed

This diagnosis uses the extracted Transformer, UMAP, and Louvain/Leiden lessons under `../skill_v0_test/` as regression evidence. Each lesson contains 28 files: lesson code, design and provenance artifacts, contracts and knowledge pack, tests, six screenshots, an audit, and a ten-file tutor directory.

The evidence supports a mixed conclusion. The lessons are technically strong, but the current skill rewards contract completeness and interactive ceremony more reliably than it rewards sustained explanation or cold-learner comprehension.

## Strengths to preserve

All three lessons are source-grounded, internally consistent, accessible, responsive, and unusually honest about test limitations:

- Their structural validator results are each `31 pass, 0 warning, 0 fail`; lesson-specific Node tests pass `9/9`, `7/7`, and `8/8`; browser audits report no failures.
- Their claim ledgers contain 10, 10, and 11 claims, all marked `verified`, backed by 7, 6, and 7 source entries respectively.
- The audits record independent formula and invariant checks, canonical-state agreement, deterministic reset, public-state events, narrow-screen behavior, keyboard paths, touch targets, reduced motion, and visual inspection.
- The audits explicitly mark real screen-reader walkthroughs, independent 200%/400% zoom review, and live provider connections as `NOT RUN` rather than inferring success.

The revision should retain provenance, mathematical verification, canonical state where representations share a model, deterministic reset for stateful interactions, accessibility, responsive review, secret safety, and honest reporting.

## Observed output tendencies

### Controls and questions dominate the page

The three static HTML files contain at least 134 buttons, inputs, selects, or textareas, plus 8 forms, 13 tables, and 20 disclosure panels. Their contracts define 10 large interactions. Their knowledge packs contain 12 diagnostic mappings, 18 concept checks, and 8 transfer questions.

This is not merely a visual impression. Across the three contracts there are exactly 40 derived representations: every one of the 10 interactions has exactly one visual, symbolic, numeric, and textual entry. They also contain 28 invariants, 20 browser paths, and 110 tutor-visible state-field references.

### Exposition is fragmented

After stripping markup, the median `<p>` length is 28 characters in Transformer, 26 in UMAP, and 22 in Louvain/Leiden. Of 207 paragraphs, 140 are at most 40 characters and only 12 exceed 80. Some are intentionally short UI messages, but the rendered pages confirm the broader pattern: definitions and mechanisms are frequently distributed among cards, captions, prompts, feedback regions, and tables rather than developed in connected teaching passages.

Representative examples include the three Q/K/V role cards in Transformer, the four pipeline cards in UMAP, and the three graph-term cards in Louvain/Leiden. These are useful summaries, but they often carry work that should also be done by a sustained explanation.

### Transformer attention is a numerical console

The main Transformer interaction, “注意力配方台,” consists of a prediction choice, four numeric K/V inputs, a mask checkbox, five stage buttons, a formula track, a six-column table, a short text summary, and reset/boundary controls. Its only SVG is a set of three weighted horizontal routes from one query to three tokens.

It does not visually construct the mechanism from tokens through Q/K/V projections, score-matrix cells, scaling and masking, row-wise softmax, and weighted value aggregation. At the default stage, weights and output remain `—` until the learner operates the stage controls. The lesson design budgets 17 of 55 minutes for reading/explanation, 28 for operation/prediction, and 10 for reflection/checking. The result is accurate and testable, but closer to a recipe workbench than an explanation of information flow.

### Still media and explanatory animation are rare

Across all three lessons there are no `<img>`, Canvas, video, or audio elements and no CSS keyframes, animation loop, `requestAnimationFrame`, or timer-driven explanatory sequence. There are nine SVG elements total; all three Louvain/Leiden SVGs and the Transformer SVG are interaction views, and only UMAP has a clearly standalone hero SVG outside an interaction.

The lessons correctly avoid decorative or inaccessible motion, but caution has collapsed into near-total absence of authored explanatory animation and limited use of standalone annotated illustration.

### The full tutor is duplicated regardless of need

Each lesson contains the same ten tutor files and five provider adapters. The three tutor directories are byte-for-byte identical and each occupies 158,691 bytes. None of the lesson learning goals requires provider switching, yet every lesson carries the complete OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, and OpenAI-compatible stack.

### Passing audits do not establish self-study effectiveness

The audit reports and browser-audit result codes cover structure, provenance, formulas, interaction paths, runtime errors, responsive layout, accessibility mechanics, public state, tutor setup, secret handling, and screenshots. They do not independently review a no-click path, explanation completeness, narrative continuity, question density, media purpose, cognitive load, or a cold learner's ability to explain the central model.

Accordingly, the existing `PASS WITH DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS` results are credible evidence of technical quality. They are not evidence that a cold learner would understand the lesson, and no actual learner study was run.

## Diagnosis

The original skill contains good pedagogical intentions—narrative spine, direct manipulation, static fallbacks, and rejection of generic dashboards—but its strongest enforcement mechanisms point elsewhere. Schemas, validators, output trees, and evaluation scores count representations, state fields, tests, paths, outcome phases, and tutor behavior. Sustained explanation, media proportionality, a coherent default story, and cold-learner comprehension are weakly specified or untested.

The revision should therefore be targeted rather than wholesale: preserve the rigorous correctness and accessibility machinery, relax fields that generate filler, make tutor and public tutor state conditional, and add independent explanation-first, no-click, proportionality, visual-purpose, and cold-walkthrough review that can fail even when all technical checks pass.
